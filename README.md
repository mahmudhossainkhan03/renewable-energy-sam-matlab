# Renewable Energy Modeling & MATLAB Systems Engineering

Techno-economic solar energy plant simulations conducted in **NREL System Advisor Model (SAM)** alongside numerical analysis, control systems, and dynamic simulations in **MATLAB & Simulink**.

---

## ☀️ Key Projects Included

### 1. Parabolic Trough Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) Plant Performance
* **Simulation Scope:** Physical parabolic trough CSP plant modeling evaluated for Arica, Chile weather conditions.
* **Key Metrics:** Configured thermal energy storage (TES) capacity, solar field collector loops, annual capacity factor, and total electrical energy generation.

### 2. Utility-Scale Photovoltaic (PV) Performance & Efficiency Analysis
* **Simulation Scope:** Performance modeling of high-capacity PV arrays, inverter clipping dynamics, and ambient temperature degradation factors.

### 3. MATLAB & Simulink Control Systems & Numerical Analysis
* **Engineering Computations:** Transfer function response modeling, differential equation solvers, matrix calculations, and dynamic control block diagrams for mechanical systems.
